Heritage & Flavor

The Roots of Northeast Indian Cooking

Northeast Indian cuisine avoids heavy oils or artificial masalas. Instead, it relies on wild herbs, natural fermentation, smoking techniques, and fiery chillies.

Fermented Bamboo Shoot & Axone

Delivers the distinct umami aroma and deep rich flavor profile signature to traditional Naga & Mizo curries.

Charcoal Skewer Roasting

Assamese Khorika skewers are marinated in mustard, garlic, and sea salt, then charcoal charred for smoky succulence.
